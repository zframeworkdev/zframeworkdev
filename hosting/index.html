Xin Chao Coffee Shop
Offline-First POS & Management System · Full-Stack Client Project

Designed and built a custom point-of-sale, inventory, reports, and employee management system for a coffee shop client in the Philippines. The core requirement: the shop keeps selling through internet outages, so the system runs on a Raspberry Pi inside the shop and syncs to the cloud whenever it's reachable, instead of depending on connectivity to take an order.

  • Offline-first architecture — local server on a Raspberry Pi, encrypted sync to a cloud VPS, no dependency on internet to sell
  • Recipe-based inventory that deducts stock automatically per sale, with low-stock alerts and wastage tracking
  • Sales reports and an owner dashboard reachable remotely, independent of the shop's own connection
  • Role-based access (Owner/Admin/Cashier), PIN-based staff attribution, and an audit trail on voids and discounts
  • ESC/POS thermal receipt printing with cash-drawer control
  • Multi-branch support with per-branch stock isolation and a cross-branch reporting switcher
