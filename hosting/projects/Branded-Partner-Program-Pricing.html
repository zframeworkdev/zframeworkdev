01 · How it's different

The same platform, launched your way

This isn't a from-scratch build — it's the same offline-first system already running in a real coffee shop: point of sale, recipe-based inventory, sales reports, and employee management, all running on a small local server inside your shop that keeps selling even when the internet doesn't.

What's different is how it's delivered. Instead of a large one-time development fee, you get your own fully rebranded instance — your logo, your colors, your shop name — live in weeks, backed by a simple monthly fee. The platform itself stays licensed rather than owned outright, which is what keeps the upfront cost low and every future improvement free.

03 · What's included

Every module, no feature cut

Nothing is held back for branded partners — you get the complete platform, the same one running a real shop today.

Point of Sale

Menu grid, live stock checks, size variants, discounts with permission control, receipt printing, void with owner PIN, end-of-day cash count.

Inventory & Recipes

Per-drink recipes that deduct stock automatically, low-stock alerts, delivery receiving, wastage tracking, monthly stock-take.

Reports & Dashboard

Daily-to-yearly sales, best sellers, sales by staff and by hour, margin per product, an owner dashboard reachable from anywhere.

Employees

Staff profiles, personal POS PINs, clock in/out, hours summary, role-based access — every action attributable to a person.

06 · Ownership & terms

The fine print, in plain words

What you own

  • Your business data — sales, inventory, and staff records — is always yours. Exportable any time, even if you cancel.
  • Your branding — logo, colors, shop name — stays yours.
  • The hardware in your shop, once purchased.

What stays with us

  • The underlying platform & source code — you're licensed to run it under your own brand, not to resell or copy it.
  • Month-to-month after setup — no long-term lock-in, cancel with 30 days' notice.
  • Updates and new features ship to every partner automatically, at no extra charge.
Want full ownership later? A branded partnership can convert into a fully-owned custom build at any time — setup fees already paid are credited toward the buyout.
