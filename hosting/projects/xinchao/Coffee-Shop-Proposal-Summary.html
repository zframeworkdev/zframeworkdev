01 · Overview

One system for the whole shop

A custom-built system with four connected modules — point of sale, inventory, sales reports, and employee management. The POS runs on ordinary tablets and phones over the shop's own Wi-Fi, served by a small local computer behind the counter. Every order deducts ingredients from stock in real time using per-drink recipes, and everything syncs to a secure cloud server so you can check your numbers from anywhere.

Because the system lives inside the shop, an internet outage never stops a sale.

02 · Features

What's inside each module

Module 1

Point of Sale

Fast enough for the morning rush, simple enough to learn in one shift.

  • Fast menu grid with categories & search
  • Live stock check before an order is accepted
  • Size variants & add-ons — extra shot, alt milk
  • Dine-in / take-out tags
  • Discounts with permission control
  • Cash & e-wallet payment recording
  • Void / refund with Owner PIN + reason
  • End-of-day cash count & shift close
Module 2

Inventory & Recipes

Every drink knows exactly what it uses. Stock counts itself as you sell.

  • Ingredient & supply catalog with units
  • Per-product recipes — e.g. latte = 18 g beans + 220 ml milk
  • Buy-unit to recipe-unit conversion (buy liters, use ml)
  • Live stock levels, deducted on every sale
  • Low-stock alerts with reorder points
  • Delivery receiving & supplier log
  • Wastage & spillage tracking
  • Expiry dates for perishables
  • Stock-take with variance report, run anytime
Module 3

Reports & Dashboard

From "how was today?" to "what should we stop selling?"

  • Daily, weekly, monthly & yearly sales, or any custom date range
  • Visual sales trend chart, not just tables
  • Best sellers & slow movers
  • Sales by hour — find your rush
  • Sales by staff member
  • Payment-type breakdown
  • Ingredient cost & gross margin per product
  • Inventory usage & wastage reports
  • Glanceable owner dashboard
  • Export to CSV / print
Module 4

Employees

One place for people, pay records, and access control.

  • Staff profiles with photo
  • Salary details — rate, allowances, notes
  • Role & personal POS PIN per staff member
  • Clock in / out at the POS
  • Hours summary per pay period
  • Documents & IDs on file
  • Active / inactive status

Every module logs who did what — voids, discounts, and stock adjustments are attributed to a person, the most effective quiet protection against shrinkage.

05 · Tech Stack

The recommended stack

LayerChoiceWhy it's right here
POS & admin apps React PWA (installable web app) One codebase runs on Android tablets, iPads, phones, and desktops. No app stores, instant updates.
Local server Raspberry Pi 4, 4 GB + PocketBase (SQLite) A silent, palm-sized computer serving the whole shop. One lightweight open-source package provides the database, sign-in, file storage, and live updates.
Cloud server Small VPS (DigitalOcean / Hetzner) Flat ~₱500/month, no per-transaction fees. Holds the synced copy, backups, and remote admin access.
Sync engine Queue-based sync service Pushes sales and stock movements up; pulls menu, price, and staff changes down. Automatic every few minutes, plus a manual "Sync now" button.
Backups Nightly encrypted off-site backups The shop's data survives fire, theft, or hardware failure.

Why not just Firebase?

Firebase is a strong platform and we evaluated it first. It falls short on this project's core requirement: true offline operation across multiple devices. Firebase's offline mode is per-device — two tablets can't agree on stock levels without internet, sign-in requires connectivity, and it can't run on the shop's own server. Its costs also scale with every read and write. The recommended stack keeps what makes Firebase attractive — one place for data, sign-in, and files — but runs it on hardware you own, with a flat, predictable cloud bill.
