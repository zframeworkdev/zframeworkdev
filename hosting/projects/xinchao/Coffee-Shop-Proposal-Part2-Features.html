03 · The modules

What's inside each module

Module 1

Point of Sale

Fast enough for the morning rush, simple enough to learn in one shift.

  • Fast menu grid with categories & search
  • Live stock check before an order is accepted
  • Size variants & add-ons — extra shot, alt milk
  • Dine-in / take-out tags
  • Discounts with permission control
  • Cash & e-wallet payment recording
  • Void / refund with Owner PIN + reason
  • End-of-day cash count & shift close
Module 2

Inventory & Recipes

Every drink knows exactly what it uses. Stock counts itself as you sell.

  • Ingredient & supply catalog with units
  • Per-product recipes — e.g. latte = 18 g beans + 220 ml milk
  • Buy-unit to recipe-unit conversion (buy liters, use ml)
  • Live stock levels, deducted on every sale
  • Low-stock alerts with reorder points
  • Delivery receiving & supplier log
  • Wastage & spillage tracking
  • Expiry dates for perishables
  • Stock-take with variance report, run anytime
Module 3

Reports & Dashboard

From "how was today?" to "what should we stop selling?"

  • Daily, weekly, monthly & yearly sales, or any custom date range
  • Visual sales trend chart, not just tables
  • Best sellers & slow movers
  • Sales by hour — find your rush
  • Sales by staff member
  • Payment-type breakdown
  • Ingredient cost & gross margin per product
  • Inventory usage & wastage reports
  • Glanceable owner dashboard
  • Export to CSV / print
Module 4

Employees

One place for people, pay records, and access control.

  • Staff profiles with photo
  • Salary details — rate, allowances, notes
  • Role & personal POS PIN per staff member
  • Clock in / out at the POS
  • Hours summary per pay period
  • Documents & IDs on file
  • Active / inactive status
04 · Foundations

Built-in safeguards

Security & access

Each staff member signs into the POS with their own PIN. Admin areas are password-protected, sync is encrypted, and remote access runs over HTTPS only.

Audit trail

Every void, discount, and stock adjustment is logged with who did it and when — quiet but effective protection against shrinkage.

Backups that restore

Nightly encrypted backups to cloud storage, and the local server runs on an SSD rather than a fragile SD card. The restore procedure is documented and tested before handover.

Room to grow

The data model is branch-aware from day one, so a second location later is an upgrade — not a rebuild. See the add-ons menu in Part 4.

05 · Who can do what

Three roles, clear boundaries

The brief named Owner and Admin. We recommend a third role — Cashier / Barista — so front-of-house staff can sell without seeing costs, salaries, or reports.
