10 · Assumptions & exclusions

The fine print, in plain words

We assume

  • Client provides the menu, recipes, prices, and staff details during discovery.
  • Single branch and a single-language (English) interface at launch.
  • Hardware is purchased by the client — we advise on models and handle all setup.
  • Cloud accounts (VPS, domain) are registered in the client's name.
  • Feedback within 3 business days at each milestone keeps the timeline.

Quoted separately if needed

  • Card / e-wallet gateway integration (see add-ons).
  • Payroll computation and payslips (salary records are included).
  • Migrating data from an existing POS system.
  • Native App Store / Play Store apps — the installable web app already covers tablets and phones.

Warranty: 30 days of free bug fixes after handover, independent of the care plan.
